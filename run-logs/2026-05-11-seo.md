## Why HowTo over Article

`BlogPosting` is a subtype of `Article` in schema.org's hierarchy — meaning the page IS already typed as an Article. Adding a parallel top-level `Article` block would be redundant. `HowTo`, by contrast, is a distinct sibling type with first-class `step` semantics — it captures structural information about the content that no `Article` variant expresses. Google retired most HowTo SERP rich results in 2023, but HowTo structured data remains valuable for AEO (AI engines parse `step` arrays to extract procedural answers).
