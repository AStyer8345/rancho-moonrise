## Findings

**1. Blog posts carry this site; landing pages do not.** 3 of 4 rankings are blog URLs. The best position in the whole set (4/8, `overnight event venue Austin`) is a blog post. The blog pipeline has been **PAUSED since 2026-04-23.**

**2. Two rankings are new to any report** — `bachelorette party Austin ranch` (5/9) and `overnight event venue Austin` (4/8). And `/weddings/` ranking on `wedding venue Austin TX ranch` is **the first core landing page ever recorded ranking** on this property.

**3. Rancho wins specific intent, loses head terms to aggregators.** Every miss is a head term owned by Glamping Hub, Hipcamp, Booking, EventUp, Cvent, Eventbrite, The Knot, ResortPass. Sharpest instance: **ResortPass holds a top-5 slot on `pool day pass Austin TX` while Rancho sells day passes on ResortPass** — Rancho reaches that SERP only as a tenant on someone else's domain, while its own `/pool-day-pass-austin/` page ranks nowhere.

**4. The queued "deepen internal links 4 → 5" work is contradicted by the data — and was not performed.** Measured across the cluster, the correlation is *inverse*: the two best performers have the **fewest** inbound links (5 each), while the most-linked page (11 inbound) doesn't rank. Word count (1.5k–2.2k) and schema depth (4–5 JSON-LD blocks) are uniform across ranking and non-ranking pages alike. CONTEXT has queued this as "mechanical, low-risk, always available" default work; it is all three, and it is also unsupported. **Retired rather than performed** — running it would have manufactured the appearance of progress.

**5. Third-party listing copy is winning the brand entity, and it is copy the brand bans.** The synthesized brand answer described the property as *"20 luxury cabins and safari tents for up to 200 guests."* `VOICE-GUIDE.md` bans **"luxury"** and bans citing a **specific unit count**. This is the `hotels-com-banned-copy` claim `rancho-competitive-weekly` has failed to verify by direct fetch **4 consecutive runs** (60s timeout). Still unconfirmed *on-page* — but now confirmed *in propagation*.
