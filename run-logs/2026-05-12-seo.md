## Why ItemList over HowTo

`HowTo` requires procedural content with explicit ordered steps where doing N+1 depends on having done N. This page is a roundup, not a procedure — the 5 destinations are independent choices, not a sequence. `ItemList` is schema.org's first-class type for ranked or curated list content (Google's recommended schema for listicle pages). `BlogPosting` is retained alongside — it's still correct as the publication-level metadata; `ItemList` is a sibling that captures the structural shape of the body content.

`itemListOrder` intentionally omitted. The numbering is partly editorial (position 1 is the host's own ranch by virtue of being a hosted brand listicle) and the rest is rough geographic order — not a competitive best-to-worst ranking. Claiming `ItemListOrderDescending` would be a SEO-bait misrepresentation. Omitting `itemListOrder` is the honest framing and Google treats it as an unordered curated list.
