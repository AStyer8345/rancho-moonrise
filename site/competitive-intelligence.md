## Section 2 — Competitor Highlights

### SERP-Layout Shift — Aggregators/Listicles Surged Across All Glamping Terms

The most consequential pattern this week is structural, not property-specific. Glamping Hub, Hipcamp, Teamout, VIP Real Estate, Wilder, and Texplorevibe listicles moved into the top slots on `glamping near austin tx`, `safari tent austin texas`, and `weekend getaway near austin glamping`. The branded competitors that held #1 last week — Udoscape, Element Ranch, Safari for the Soul — all slid beneath that stack in the same week. Three #1-holders losing the top slot simultaneously points to a SERP-presentation change favoring listicles rather than three independent ranking losses. **Implication for Rancho:** the head terms are increasingly won through inclusion in the listicles/aggregators (Hipcamp curated set, Glamping Hub) rather than purely through on-site SEO — which makes the long-standing Glamping Hub absence (9 weeks) and Hipcamp curation gap (5 reads) more material, not less.

### Material State Change — Hipcamp Curated Set Rotated (Streak Broken)

**The 6/8 "byte-identical, zero rotation" read was a one-week anomaly.** Hipcamp's "20 Best Glamping Near Austin" set rotated this week — Urban Hideout (#1) and Cozy Cactus Airstream (#2) anchor the new top, and **Lucky Arrow slipped from position 8 to position 9.** Rancho Moonrise remains absent across all 5 reads since the 5/16 audit. The takeaway is unchanged: the curated set is a real, recurring inclusion gap, and the Ashley strategy question is the cleanest way to retire it.

### Material State Change — The Yurtopian Re-Entered the Corporate-Retreat SERP

After being off the `corporate retreat near austin texas` top 10 for four consecutive weeks, **The Yurtopian re-entered at ~#3.** Its page never changed (10 Dripping Springs yurts, 46-acre resort, corporate-retreats path in nav under "Events & Groups") — this is pure SERP movement, consistent with the cluster's weekly churn. In the same shuffle Sage Hill took #1, Element Ranch fell to ~#6, and Lucky Arrow + Crystal Creek + Moniker Partners dropped off while Peaceful Waters + 7744 Ranch held.

### Spoon Mountain Lost Ground on Both Its Terms

Spoon Mountain dropped off the safari-tent SERP entirely (it was a new ~#5 entrant on 6/8) and slipped from #1 to ~#5 on the narrow "romantic weekend getaways near austin," displaced above by travel-editorial (Visit Austin, VacationIdea, Yelp). It's still the only glamping *property* on page 1 of the narrow term — the long-tail-research-content read holds — but even that slot is now contested by general travel-editorial, which softens the "research-intent content reliably wins narrow intent" framing.
