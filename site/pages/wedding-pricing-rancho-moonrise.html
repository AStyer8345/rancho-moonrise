What Drives Wedding Pricing?

Published November 16, 2026 · 3 min read

Wedding pricing changes with the details of your celebration. Your date, guest count, bar service, lodging plans, and vendor choices all affect the final quote. Here is how to think through those pieces before you request pricing for a wedding at Rancho Moonrise.

Your date and length of stay

Venue rental at Rancho Moonrise starts at $2,000+, with pricing varying by day of the week and season. A single wedding day and a wedding weekend involve different plans for use of the ranch and overnight stays. If you have flexibility, share a few possible dates so we can walk you through the available options.

Spring and fall Saturdays are popular wedding dates in Central Texas. Touring early gives you more time to compare dates and build a plan around the spaces you want to use.

Guest count and event layout

The number of guests shapes your ceremony seating, reception layout, staffing, and bar needs. Rancho Moonrise has indoor and outdoor celebration spaces, including the Event Barn, Neon Moon Bar & Lounge, pool area, corral, and open fields. We can talk through the flow that fits your group when you tour the property.

Bar service and lodging

Alcohol service is handled through Rancho Moonrise. We can discuss bar packages based on your guest count, drink choices, and service hours; outside alcohol and BYOB are not available. On-site casitas and safari tents can also be part of a wedding weekend or a stay for close friends and family. The lodging and bar plans are priced alongside your venue needs.

Vendors and what's included

Catering, photography, and florals remain flexible, so you can bring your preferred vendors. The venue includes key essentials such as ceremony benches, reception chairs, guest and vendor parking, and a day-of venue manager. Review the current What's Included section for the full list, then ask which additional services your vendors will provide.

How to get an accurate quote

Send your preferred date range, estimated guest count, and whether you are considering lodging and bar service. Taxes and fees are separate from the starting venue rental price. A tour is the easiest way to discuss the spaces and build a quote for the wedding you actually want.
