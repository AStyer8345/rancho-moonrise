An Event Venue Near Austin Built for What You're Planning

Rancho Moonrise is a 36-acre event property 20 minutes east of downtown Austin. The climate-controlled Event Barn holds up to 200 guests, with a resort-style pool and open pastures under the Texas sky. Available for hourly rentals, partial bookings, or full ranch buyouts — with on-site overnight accommodations across cabins and safari tents.

Whether you're hosting a corporate off-site for 30 or a festival for 200, we'll build the setup around your event.

What to Know

What Drives Event Pricing

Event pricing depends on date, guest count, bar package, lodging needs, and how much of the ranch you want for your event. Small gatherings start with an hourly rental while larger parties move into private ranch buyouts with overnight stays. Bar service is available through the venue with flexible packages to fit your event — reach out and we'll walk you through options.

A planning call or tour is the fastest way to get an accurate quote — we'll match you to the right setup based on what you're trying to pull off.
