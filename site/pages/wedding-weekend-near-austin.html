How to Plan a Wedding Weekend Near Austin with On-Site Lodging

Published November 17, 2026 · 5 min read

A wedding weekend gives you time for the conversations that rarely fit between the ceremony and the last dance. Instead of greeting everyone once and wishing you had longer, you can plan a welcome gathering, celebrate your wedding, and see your closest people again before they head home.

At Rancho Moonrise, a private ranch wedding venue about 20 minutes from downtown Austin, the setting brings outdoor celebration spaces and on-site casitas and safari tents together on 36 acres. Here is how to turn that possibility into a weekend that feels relaxed and works for your guests.

Start with the people who will stay overnight

Your reception guest list and overnight guest list do not have to be the same. Decide who you would most like nearby: your wedding party, immediate family, or a smaller group of friends. Then ask our team which accommodations are available for your dates and how their bed and bathroom configurations fit that group.

Our casitas and safari tents have real beds, A/C, and heat. Casitas use a shared bathhouse, while safari tent bathroom arrangements vary by room type. Share those details before guests commit, especially if someone needs a particular bathroom setup or accessible route.

Confirm what your private weekend includes

Full-ranch exclusivity is part of the Rancho Moonrise wedding experience. Your agreement should also make clear exactly when that private access begins and ends. A wedding-day booking and a multi-day celebration need different arrangements.

Before sending invitations, confirm lodging costs, the number of nights, setup and teardown access, bar service, and any extra gatherings. Ask how overnight reservations and payments will be handled. Keeping those decisions together makes it easier to build an accurate budget and give guests clear instructions.

A sample Friday-to-Sunday wedding itinerary

This is a planning example, not a package or a list of included services. Extra days, meals, gatherings, and activities must be arranged with the venue and your vendors.

Friday: arrive and settle in

Leave room for travel delays and time to find accommodations. If your agreement includes a rehearsal, schedule it with the venue before finalizing the evening. A welcome gathering can be a lovely way to introduce both sides of the family; confirm its location, catering, bar arrangements, and end time separately.

Keep the first evening simple. Guests should know what is hosted, what is optional, and whether they need dinner before arriving.

Saturday: leave breathing room around the celebration

Build the morning around hair and makeup, getting dressed, photography, and vendor arrivals. Rancho offers separate getting-ready spaces, so walk through those rooms during your tour and share the plan with your photographer and beauty team.

Choose a ceremony location and map the route to cocktails and the reception. The circular corral, poolside areas, open fields, and Event Barn offer different ways to arrange the day. Confirm the indoor weather plan for your actual guest count before assigning spaces.

Leave a short pause for the two of you after the ceremony. It also helps to appoint one person to answer vendor questions so you are not coordinating deliveries while taking portraits.

Sunday: make departures easy

If you would like a farewell breakfast or another final gathering, arrange the catering and space in advance. Keep it optional for guests with early travel plans. Share the agreed checkout time, where to return keys, and who is collecting personal items and decor.

Send guests one clear weekend guide

Include arrival instructions, room assignments or booking instructions, bathroom details, meal plans, dress suggestions for outdoor areas, and a contact for practical questions. Guests staying elsewhere will also need a transport plan. On-site lodging can simplify the weekend for some of your guests; it does not replace planning for everyone else.

For a fuller list of lodging questions, read our wedding venue lodging checklist. For budget considerations, see what drives wedding pricing at Rancho Moonrise.

Walk the weekend before you book it

A private tour lets you picture the whole flow: where people arrive, where you get ready, where you celebrate, and where your closest guests sleep. Bring your date range and estimated guest count, and we can discuss what fits your plans.
