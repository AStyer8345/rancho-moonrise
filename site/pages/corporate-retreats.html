What Is a Corporate Retreat at Rancho Moonrise?

Rancho Moonrise is a 36-acre ranch venue 20 minutes east of downtown Austin, built for corporate groups that want to step out of the office without booking a flight. The Event Barn seats 200 for presentations, all-hands, and working sessions. On-site lodging — cabins and safari tents — keeps the team together overnight. The pool, fire pits, and open ranch land handle everything outside the structured agenda.

Rancho Moonrise hosts corporate groups from 20 to 200, with full and partial property buyouts available. Pricing is custom by group size, duration, lodging configuration, bar service, and catering — every retreat is built around the team's agenda, not a fixed package.

Pricing

What Drives Corporate Retreat Pricing

Pricing is custom because every retreat is built around the team's agenda — not the other way around. A 25-person leadership offsite for a single afternoon and a 150-person all-hands across three days are different jobs. The package is scoped during the tour.

Five inputs drive the number:

  • Group size — more people, more space, more service. Range is 20 to 200.
  • Duration — single-day, two-day, or multi-day. Day-of vs. multi-night changes lodging completely.
  • Lodging configuration — partial buyout (block of rooms) vs. full property buyout (private use of the ranch).
  • Bar service — open bar is priced per-person per-hour and provided through the venue. Outside alcohol is not permitted on the property.
  • Catering — coordinated with vendors the ranch team works with. Quote varies by menu and headcount.

The tour walks the venue, gets the agenda on paper, and turns into a real quote — not a rate card.
