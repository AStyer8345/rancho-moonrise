What Is a Ranch Wedding Venue Near Austin?

Published November 16, 2026 · 3 min read

A ranch wedding venue near Austin gives couples room to spread out: outdoor ceremony options, a setting with its own character, and space for the celebration to move from vows to dinner to dancing. The details that matter most are how much of the property is yours, what happens if the weather changes, and whether guests can stay nearby.

What makes a ranch venue different?

At Rancho Moonrise, couples have exclusive use of the 36-acre ranch for their wedding. There are no outside guests on the property during the event, so your wedding party can move between the ceremony, cocktail hour, reception, and outdoor gathering spaces without sharing the venue with another event.

The setting offers several ways to make the day your own: exchange vows in the circular corral, by the pool, in an open field, or inside the Event Barn. The barn also offers a climate-controlled option when an indoor plan makes more sense. These spaces let you shape the flow of the day around your guest count and the atmosphere you want.

Can guests stay on the ranch?

Yes. Rancho Moonrise has on-site casitas and safari tents for overnight guests. Keeping your wedding party on the property can simplify getting-ready plans and give close friends and family more time together after the reception. The pool, Neon Moon Bar & Lounge, Lodge, and open spaces are all part of the ranch setting.

How close is it to Austin?

Rancho Moonrise is about 20 minutes from downtown Austin. It offers a Texas ranch setting while remaining convenient for Austin-area vendors and guests traveling from the city.

What should you ask on a venue tour?

Walk through the actual spaces your guests will use. Ask where the ceremony and reception would go for your guest count, what the weather backup looks like, which furniture and staffing are included, and how lodging and bar service fit into your plans. Those details help you compare the full wedding experience, not just the venue photos.
