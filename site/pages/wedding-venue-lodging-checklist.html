An Austin Wedding Venue with Lodging: What to Ask Before You Book

Published November 17, 2026 · 5 min read

A wedding venue with on-site lodging can give you more time with the people you love. It can also make getting ready and returning to your room simpler. Before you choose a property, look closely at how the overnight arrangements will work for your particular guest list.

At Rancho Moonrise, about 20 minutes from downtown Austin, couples can bring their celebration and overnight stays together on a private 36-acre ranch. Our casitas and safari tents offer a different experience from a hotel room block. These are the questions we recommend bringing to your venue tour.

1. How many of our guests can actually stay?

Event capacity and sleeping capacity are separate numbers. Start with the people who want to stay, then ask for the accommodation inventory available for your dates. Check actual bed arrangements rather than assuming every room works for every guest.

A couple sharing a bed, a family with children, and friends who need separate beds may all need different options. Ask which layouts are available and how room assignments will be confirmed before you promise accommodations to anyone.

2. Which rooms have private bathrooms?

This detail deserves a clear answer before booking. At Rancho Moonrise, casitas use a shared bathhouse. Safari tents have different configurations, including shared-bathhouse and private ensuite options. Review the current accommodation descriptions and confirm your selected rooms with our team.

Ask to see the bathrooms and the paths between them and the rooms. Explain the arrangement on your wedding website so guests can choose what suits them.

3. What does the room price include?

Ask whether lodging is included in the venue agreement or charged separately, whether a minimum stay applies, and what taxes or fees are additional. Confirm whether the couple pays for the rooms or guests can reserve and pay individually.

Also ask about payment deadlines, cancellation terms, and responsibility for any rooms that remain unbooked. An attractive nightly rate tells only part of the story; the full commitment should be clear in writing.

4. When is the property private?

Rancho Moonrise weddings offer exclusive ranch access during the agreed event period. If you are planning a whole weekend, confirm the beginning and end of that private access, including any welcome gathering or farewell plans.

Ask which spaces are included and how vendor setup, guest arrivals, and departures fit the schedule. The answer should reflect your booking rather than an assumption that an extra overnight stay automatically includes another event.

5. Will the accommodations work for our guests?

Walk the routes your guests will use between parking, rooms, bathrooms, and celebration spaces. Ask specific questions about surfaces, steps, lighting, and distances for anyone with mobility needs. Discuss individual accessibility requirements directly with the venue.

Rancho's casitas and safari tents have A/C, heat, and real beds. Guests can check our guest FAQs for practical stay details, then contact the team about any needs that require an individual arrangement.

6. Can guests bring their dogs?

Pet-friendly wedding policies and pet-friendly rooms are not always the same. At Rancho Moonrise, dogs are welcome in safari tents under the current pet policy; casitas are not dog friendly. Confirm eligible rooms, fees, and restrictions before inviting a guest to bring a dog.

If your own dog will join the ceremony, plan who will care for them afterward and discuss the arrangements with the venue.

7. What happens for guests staying elsewhere?

Some guests may prefer a hotel or need a different room setup. Give them clear directions, the event schedule, and transport information. If you arrange a shuttle, confirm pickup locations, loading access, and the final departure with both the venue and transport provider.

Do not assume everyone will be able to walk back to a room after the reception. Plan the evening around both overnight guests and people leaving the property.

8. Which weekend gatherings need separate arrangements?

A welcome party, pool gathering, rehearsal dinner, or farewell meal can make the weekend feel connected. Each also needs a plan for space, staffing, food, drinks, and timing. Ask what is possible under your agreement and what costs extra.

Alcohol at Rancho Moonrise must be arranged through the venue; outside alcohol is not permitted. Confirm bar service for each hosted gathering rather than assuming reception service covers the entire stay.

Bring these details to your private tour

Have your estimated reception guest count, likely overnight guest list, preferred number of nights, room needs, and any accessibility questions ready. A good tour should help you picture the experience and understand the commitments before you sign.
