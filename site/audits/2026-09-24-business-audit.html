2. Website

The Sept 16 Repair Was the Biggest Single Site Gain Since DNS Cutover:

Before and after on the live homepage (mobile Lighthouse, same config): performance 70 → 99, accessibility 93 → 100, SEO 92 → 100, LCP 5.4s → 2.1s, FCP 2.9s → 1.1s. What changed: photos were re-encoded (about 33% smaller across the asset set, with a 768px phone size), the homepage now opens on real cabins, mislabeled alt text was corrected, and nested /blog/ pages that had been 404ing their own CSS and JS were fixed. The runtime moved to Node 24 ahead of Vercel's Oct 1 Node 20 cutoff. Today ranchomoonrise.com and rancho-moonrise.vercel.app both returned HTTP 200, server: Vercel.

The Site Now Ranks and Gets Cited:

At baseline, Rancho sat in the top 10 on 0 of 10 tracked non-brand keywords. The 9/20 metrics pull recorded 4 of 10: "corporate retreat Austin" #3 and #4 (landing page + blog), "glamping near Austin with pool" #6, and "bachelorette party Austin ranch" #6. The 9/21 competitive read had 3 of 10, because the wedding head term slipped off the tail. Today's AI-answer tests: "best glamping near Austin with pool" named Rancho first. "Corporate retreat venue near Austin ranch" also named Rancho first and returned two owned URLs. "Best wedding venues near Austin 2026" did not cite Rancho. Weddings are still the gap. /corporate-retreats/, flagged as uncrawled in June, is now indexed and ranking.

Visible Defects Still Live:

Three issues a visitor or Google can see today. (1) The "Escape to Rancho Moonrise" card on /videos/ still points at a removed YouTube video. The thumbnail returned 404 this morning, and it's been open since July 23. (2) The homepage still says "125+ Google reviews" and its schema says reviewCount: 125, but the last direct read was 130. (3) /host-your-event/ says the barn holds 200 in one place and "up to 1,000+ guests" in the FAQ. Search engines see both numbers. Separately, /safari-tents-near-austin/ is still missing from every site: read (~150 days). On-site causes were ruled out 8/31. The only remaining fix is the GSC URL Inspection request.

Next Actions:

1) GSC → URL Inspection → Request Indexing on /safari-tents-near-austin/ (2 min). 2) Remove or replace the dead video card, and pick the real event capacity so the events page stops contradicting itself (one decision each). 3) Re-upload the 4 accommodation photos that are 340×340 at 1600px+. They're the last cap on image quality.
